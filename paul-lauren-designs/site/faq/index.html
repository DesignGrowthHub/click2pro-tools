FAQ

Questions, answered

About the studio, Lauren's approach and how a project works. Can't find what you're looking for? Ask us directly.

Who is the designer behind Paul Lauren Designs?

Paul Lauren Designs is led by principal designer and founder Lauren Rautbord, who has more than 30 years of interior design experience. She trained at the Harrington School of Design in Chicago and founded the firm with the late Paul Marchetti, one of Chicago's leading style makers for decades.

Where is Paul Lauren Designs located?

The studio is at 16419 N 91st St, Bldg A125, Scottsdale, AZ 85260, in North Scottsdale. Appointments can be made by calling 480-664-6765 or emailing info@paullaurendesigns.com.

What areas does Paul Lauren Designs serve?

The studio works throughout Scottsdale, Paradise Valley and Phoenix, including Silverleaf, DC Ranch and the Biltmore area, and has long-standing clients in Chicago and the North Shore. It has also completed homes in Aspen, Sun Valley, Coeur d'Alene and Pacific Palisades.

What is Lauren Rautbord's design style?

Serene, uncluttered and quietly luxurious: sumptuous fabrics, refined neutral palettes and furnishings full of warmth and personality, with vintage and contemporary pieces side by side. The guiding beliefs are that less is always more and that a home's views should be embraced.

Do you take on new construction and renovation projects?

Yes. The studio works alongside architects and builders on new homes and renovations, including interior architecture, kitchens and baths, cabinetry and millwork, finishes, lighting and furnishings.

Can you design a second home if I live out of state?

Yes. Many projects are vacation and second homes, including mountain cabins, lake houses and desert retreats. The studio can design and fully outfit a home so it is ready to live in on arrival.

How involved is Lauren in each project?

Personally involved in every project. The studio stays deliberately small so that design decisions are never delegated or made 'off the shelf'. Clients work directly with the designer.

How does the design process work?

Six stages: an introductory conversation, discovery and site visit, a design concept, detailed design development, procurement and project oversight, and finally installation and the reveal. Each stage is explained on the Process page.

How long does an interior design project take?

It depends on scope. Furnishing a finished home typically takes a matter of months, mostly lead time for custom pieces, while new construction follows the build schedule. A realistic timeline is part of the first conversation.

How do I start a project with Paul Lauren Designs?

Use the contact form, email info@paullaurendesigns.com or call 480-664-6765. Share a little about your home, your timeline and what you would like to achieve, and the studio will arrange an introductory conversation.

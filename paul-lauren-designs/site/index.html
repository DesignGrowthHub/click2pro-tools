Philosophy

Philosophy

Less is always more.

  1. 01

    Less, always

    Rooms are edited until only what matters remains. Uncluttered space is the true luxury, and it lets every piece be seen.

  2. 02

    Sumptuous, never fussy

    Luxurious fabrics, a refined neutral palette and furnishings with warmth and personality: sophisticated, comfortable and made to be lived in.

  3. 03

    Embrace the view

    Desert, mountain, lake or skyline, the setting is part of the design. Interiors are composed to frame the outlook rather than compete with it.

  4. 04

    Vintage meets contemporary

    Collected vintage pieces and contemporary design sit side by side, giving each home a sense of history and a point of view.

“The day I am delegating design decisions to others or hurriedly making selections ‘off the shelf’ will be the day I know we’ve become too large.”

— Lauren Rautbord, Principal Designer

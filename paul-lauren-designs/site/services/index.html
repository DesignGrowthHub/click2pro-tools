Services

From first sketch to final pillow

Full-service interior design for new construction, renovation and furnishing in Scottsdale, Paradise Valley and Phoenix, and for second homes across the West.

Full-Service Interior Design
01

Full-Service Interior Design

From the first conversation to the final pillow, one designer guiding every decision.

Space planning, interior architecture and finish selections, custom furnishings, lighting, window treatments, art and accessories, all designed, specified and managed by the studio, with Lauren personally involved in every selection.

  • Space planning & furniture layouts
  • Finish, fixture & material selections
  • Custom furniture & upholstery
  • Lighting & window treatments
  • Art & accessory curation
New Construction & Renovation
02

New Construction & Renovation

An interior point of view from the plans stage onward.

Working alongside your architect and builder, we shape cabinetry, millwork, stone, tile, plumbing and lighting so the architecture and the interiors read as one, whether it is a new build in Silverleaf or a mountain-home renovation.

  • Plan review with architect & builder
  • Kitchen & bath design
  • Cabinetry & millwork details
  • Stone, tile & flooring
  • Lighting plans
Furnishing & Styling
03

Furnishing & Styling

For finished homes ready for their interior layer.

A complete furnishing plan, from upholstery and case goods to rugs, linens and objects, sourced from trade-only showrooms and artisans, then installed and styled so the home is ready to live in.

  • Furniture plans & sourcing
  • Custom upholstery & textiles
  • Rugs, linens & bedding
  • Installation & styling
Second Homes & Turnkey Retreats
04

Second Homes & Turnkey Retreats

Arrive to a home that is ready: linens pressed, lamps on.

Mountain cabins, lake houses and desert retreats designed and fully outfitted down to the kitchen, bath and bedding, ideal for clients who live elsewhere. The studio has completed homes in Aspen, Sun Valley, Coeur d'Alene and beyond.

  • Complete outfitting to move-in
  • Remote collaboration
  • Kitchen, bath & linen packages
  • Seasonal refreshes
Builder & Spec Home Design
05

Builder & Spec Home Design

Design that helps a home sell before it is finished.

Finish selections and furnishing for builders and developers, creating a warm, personal sense of home that buyers respond to.

  • Finish & fixture packages
  • Model & spec furnishing
  • Staging-level styling
Design Consultation
06

Design Consultation

Expert direction for a single room or a single decision.

Focused sessions for clients who want a professional eye on a layout, palette, furniture plan or finish selection.

  • In-home or virtual sessions
  • Palette & material direction
  • Layout & furniture planning

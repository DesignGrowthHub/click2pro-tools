Interior design for the way the desert lives

Designing in Scottsdale means designing with the light. Clear mornings, white-bright middays and long golden evenings across the McDowell Mountains all shape how a room feels. Lauren Rautbord's interiors are composed for that light: refined neutral palettes that glow rather than glare, sumptuous natural textures, and furniture arranged so the view is part of every room.

The studio works on new construction alongside the Valley's architects and builders, on renovations, and on complete furnishing projects. Its Scottsdale work includes residences in Silverleaf, a modern ranch, a Mediterranean home and a transitional one, and across town a Biltmore residence in Phoenix.

Why clients choose Paul Lauren Designs

  • Direct access to the principal designer on every decision
  • More than thirty years of experience, from Chicago to the desert

Questions

Working with a Scottsdale designer

Where is Paul Lauren Designs located?

The studio is at 16419 N 91st St, Bldg A125, Scottsdale, AZ 85260, in North Scottsdale. Appointments can be made by calling 480-664-6765 or emailing info@paullaurendesigns.com.

What areas does Paul Lauren Designs serve?

The studio works throughout Scottsdale, Paradise Valley and Phoenix, including Silverleaf, DC Ranch and the Biltmore area, and has long-standing clients in Chicago and the North Shore. It has also completed homes in Aspen, Sun Valley, Coeur d'Alene and Pacific Palisades.

Do you take on new construction and renovation projects?

Yes. The studio works alongside architects and builders on new homes and renovations, including interior architecture, kitchens and baths, cabinetry and millwork, finishes, lighting and furnishings.

How do I start a project with Paul Lauren Designs?

Use the contact form, email info@paullaurendesigns.com or call 480-664-6765. Share a little about your home, your timeline and what you would like to achieve, and the studio will arrange an introductory conversation.

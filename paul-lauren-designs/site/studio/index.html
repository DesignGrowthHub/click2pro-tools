A studio that stays personal

Paul Lauren Designs began in Chicago, where Lauren founded the firm with the late Paul Marchetti, for decades one of the city's leading style makers. Lauren trained at the Harrington School of Design and built a loyal clientele across the Upper Midwest before bringing the studio to Scottsdale, where it now designs homes throughout the Valley and in mountain and lake towns across the West.

Her rooms are recognizable for their calm. Luxurious fabrics, refined neutral palettes and furnishings full of warmth and personality come together in spaces that are sophisticated but never fussy. Vintage and contemporary pieces sit easily side by side. Lauren holds that less is always more and that a home's views should be embraced and enjoyed.

The studio is deliberately small. Every client works directly with Lauren, and every selection is considered, never hurried or made off the shelf.

“The day I am delegating design decisions to others or hurriedly making selections ‘off the shelf’ will be the day I know we’ve become too large.”

— Lauren Rautbord
Studio

Philosophy

What guides every room

  1. 01

    Less, always

    Rooms are edited until only what matters remains. Uncluttered space is the true luxury, and it lets every piece be seen.

  2. 02

    Sumptuous, never fussy

    Luxurious fabrics, a refined neutral palette and furnishings with warmth and personality: sophisticated, comfortable and made to be lived in.

  3. 03

    Embrace the view

    Desert, mountain, lake or skyline, the setting is part of the design. Interiors are composed to frame the outlook rather than compete with it.

  4. 04

    Vintage meets contemporary

    Collected vintage pieces and contemporary design sit side by side, giving each home a sense of history and a point of view.

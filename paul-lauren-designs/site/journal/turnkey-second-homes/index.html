Furnishing a Second Home: Mountain Cabins, Lake Houses & Desert Retreats

How to design and fully outfit a second home, whether a mountain cabin, lake house or desert retreat, so it's ready the day you arrive.

By Paul Lauren Designs · July 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Design for how the house will really be used: guests, seasons and long weekends.
  • Choose durable, easy-care materials, since sand, snow, lake water and sun all take a toll.
  • A true turnkey install covers kitchen, bath, linens and the small things, not just furniture.

Design for the life you'll live there

A second home has a different rhythm from a primary residence. Weekends fill with guests, summers with family, ski seasons with wet boots. Before choosing a single sofa we map how the house will actually be used: how many people sleep over, where everyone gathers, and what happens on a rainy day.

The answers shape everything, from bunk rooms and oversized dining tables to mudrooms that handle gear gracefully and outdoor rooms that extend the living space.

Materials that take on the elements

Lake houses meet wet swimsuits, mountain homes meet snow and mud, and desert retreats meet relentless sun. Performance fabrics, washable slipcovers, wool rugs, oiled woods and honed stone look relaxed and stay beautiful under real use. The aim is a home that feels luxurious but never precious.

Let the setting lead

Whether the view is of a lake, a fairway or the Sawtooths, the interior should defer to it. Warm woods, natural textures and an edited palette connect the inside to the landscape, and furniture is placed so the best seats face the view. It's the same principle the studio brings to every home: embrace the view.

What turnkey really means

For clients who live elsewhere, the luxury is arriving to a home that is completely ready. A turnkey installation goes well beyond furniture to cover cookware and dinnerware, glassware, bath linens and robes, bedding, books, lamps on timers and fresh flowers on the table. The studio coordinates delivery, installation and styling remotely and keeps the client informed throughout.

Paul Lauren Designs has created mountain and lake homes in Aspen, Sun Valley and Coeur d'Alene as well as desert retreats in the Valley, so the details are second nature.

Our Process

A calm, considered process

Beautiful homes come from clear conversations and careful decisions. Here is how a project with Paul Lauren Designs unfolds.

  1. 01

    Introduction

    A conversation about your home, how you live, your timeline and your goals. We'll talk through scope and whether we're the right fit.

  2. 02

    Discovery & Site Visit

    We walk the home or the plans, measure, photograph and listen. We look closely at how the light moves, what the views offer and which pieces you love.

  3. 03

    Concept

    A design direction in plan, palette, materials and key furnishings, presented in person so every choice can be seen and touched.

  4. 04

    Design Development

    Detailed selections: finishes, lighting, custom pieces, fabrics, rugs and window treatments, refined with you until each room is resolved.

  5. 05

    Procurement & Oversight

    The studio orders, tracks and receives everything, coordinates with your architect, builder and trades, and keeps the details on course.

  6. 06

    Installation & Reveal

    Furniture is placed, art is hung, beds are made and accessories are styled, then we hand you the keys to a finished home.

Questions

Frequently asked

Will I work directly with the designers?

Yes. The studio stays deliberately small so that clients work directly with Lauren and Kendra, and design decisions are never delegated or made 'off the shelf'.

How does the design process work?

Six stages: an introductory conversation, discovery and site visit, a design concept, detailed design development, procurement and project oversight, and finally installation and the reveal. Each stage is explained on the Process page.

How long does an interior design project take?

It depends on scope. Furnishing a finished home typically takes a matter of months, mostly lead time for custom pieces, while new construction follows the build schedule. A realistic timeline is part of the first conversation.

How do I start a project with Paul Lauren Designs?

Use the contact form, email info@paullaurendesigns.com or call 480-664-6765. Share a little about your home, your timeline and what you would like to achieve, and the studio will arrange an introductory conversation.
